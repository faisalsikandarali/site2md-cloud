## 1. Single Purpose Overview
Site2MD has a single, transparent purpose: to extract the public visual design tokens, computed CSS styles, colors, typography, animations, and layout structure of the active webpage and compile them into a structured Markdown prompt for AI coding workflows.

## 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
Site2MD operates **100% client-side** inside your local browser tab:
- **No Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** We do NOT collect your name, email, IP address, device specs, or location.
- **No Browsing History Tracking:** We do NOT record, log, or transmit the websites you visit or your browsing activity.
- **No Credential Access:** Site2MD does NOT interact with passwords, session tokens, cookies, or authentication details.
- **No Keystroke or Mouse Tracking:** We do NOT record your keystrokes, clicks, or mouse positions.

## 3. Local Storage Usage
Site2MD uses Chrome's native `chrome.storage.local` API exclusively on your device for:
- Tracking your local scan count (for quota enforcement).
- Storing your offline activated license key (if applicable).
- Remembering dismissed sponsor notifications.
None of this information is transmitted to external servers.

## 4. Third-Party Network Requests
Site2MD communicates exclusively via secure HTTPS with:
- `raw.githubusercontent.com`: Fetches public, read-only JSON lists of active sponsor cards and valid license signatures. No user data is sent.
- `api.lemonsqueezy.com`: Used strictly when verifying an optional license key entered by the user.

## 5. Certification & Compliance
We certify that:
1. We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
2. We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
3. We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
